The torrent action after a rename is now a setting, defaulting to Remove. Media Settings gains 'Torrents whose files were moved' with three choices: Remove the torrent (the default), Report it and change nothing, or Do nothing. It is a separate card from 'Junk file removal' on purpose — that one deletes junk FILES and never touches video, this one governs the torrent ENTRY in the download engine, and folding them together would rebuild exactly the one-word-two-features confusion that made 'cleanup' ambiguous. Remove is the default because doing nothing is not neutral: a torrent whose files moved errors out and, on its next recheck, downloads the whole release again beside the copy just organised. The consequence of each choice is written where the choice is made rather than in documentation nobody opens. Report is the middle ground for an operator who wants to decide each one, and it is also what a 'remove' policy DEGRADES to when the library has not opted into automatic organising — reporting is non-destructive, so an orphaned torrent is surfaced in the job log instead of accumulating unnoticed. Both counts are returned by the rename and recorded on its audit row (torrentsRemoved, torrentsOrphaned). Full en-US and es-PR strings.
